3. OBSERVATIONAL SCIENTIFIC RIGOR & CAUSAL LANGUAGE AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

While Section 7 of eda_report.txt explicitly emphasizes that correlation does not
imply causation, several sentences in earlier summary sections and figure suptitles
use language that could inadvertently imply causal or mechanistic relationships.

Flagged Items & Recommended Observational Corrections:

1. Term: "Causal Explanation" (Step 4 summary, Section 2)
   - Current: "*Causal Explanation:* Remedial support does not cause lower achievement. Rather,
     prior academic struggle triggers institutional placement into support programs..."
   - Audit Note: In observational cross-sectional data without longitudinal timestamps of support
     entry, this is a plausible confounding hypothesis rather than an empirically proven causal mechanism.
   - Recommended Correction: Replace with "*Selection Bias / Confounding by Indication Hypothesis*".

2. Term: "Impact" (Figures 11 & 14 titles, eda_report.txt Section 3.C)
   - Current Figure 11 Title: "Academic Effort & Historical Burden: Impact of Study Hours and Past Failures on G3"
   - Current Figure 14 Title: "Impact of Environmental, Support, and Lifestyle Categorical Factors on Mean Final Grade (G3)"
   - Audit Note: "Impact" denotes an active intervention or causal treatment effect.
   - Recommended Correction: Replace with "*Relationship of Study Hours and Past Failures with Final Grade*"
     and "*Distribution of Mean Final Grade Across Environmental, Support, and Lifestyle Categories*".

3. Term: "The Aspiration Premium" / "+3.81 point premium" (Summary Section 2 & eda_report.txt Section 5.2)
   - Current: "The Aspiration Premium... +3.81 point premium"
   - Audit Note: "Premium" implies a guaranteed return on investment.
   - Recommended Correction: Use "*Mean Performance Advantage*" or "*Observed Difference in Group Means*".

4. Term: "Predictor" (Multiple sections)
   - Current: "Dominant proximal predictor", "Strongest categorical predictor"
   - Audit Note: In machine learning, "predictor" is standard for feature X. However, in pure EDA,
     it can blur the distinction with temporal or causal prediction.
   - Recommended Correction: Use "*Strongest Associated Categorical Factor*" or "*Feature Most Strongly Correlated*".

5. Term: "Course Dropouts / Exam Disqualification" (Sections analyzing G3 = 0)
   - Current: Interpreted as definitive course dropouts or examination disqualifications.
   - Audit Note: The dataset does not contain an administrative dropout status column. While this
     is the universally accepted consensus among educational researchers who benchmark this UCI dataset,
     rigorous scientific reporting must label this as an "*Inferred Characteristic / Hypothesized Structural Dropout*".
